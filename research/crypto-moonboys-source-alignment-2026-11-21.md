## Source priority when these sites disagree

1. **Present-day brand and products:** use current Crypto Moonboys README, Master Source of Truth, current public creator guide, published licence terms and observed runtime. Do not treat an older whitepaper or archive pitch as proof of present capabilities.
2. **Fictional canon:** use the approved decision and official canon index, then the owning public wiki text. An older wiki copy can lag behind a dated, approved canon decision. No real person inherits a fictional rank or family role.
3. **Charlie Buster's real art:** preserve NO BALL GAMES' artist and project records, sourced artwork catalogue and public interview links. The connected Moonboys universe does not replace an independent artwork's authorship or provenance.
4. **Rights and reward claims:** published asset-specific terms and live, verifiable contracts control eligibility. A generic NFT, fictional story, XP figure or campaign brief does not prove a guaranteed payout, copyright transfer or 1/1 licence.
