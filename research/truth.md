The stronger claims about Charlie Buster, Graffiti Kings employment/history, No Ball Games NFTs, WAXP activity, and the $NBG token should be split into two classes:

1. **Publicly verified / safe to publish** — supported by public website, source files, known repo state, or credible public references.
2. **Founder-supplied / needs external proof** — usable internally, but not phrased as fact to the public until backed by public links, screenshots, blockchain explorer pages, marketplace pages, contract links, collection pages, or signed statements.

This file is deliberately strict. It protects the brand from fake-looking hype and makes future SEO content more credible.

## 1. Verification labels

Use these labels across all future content drafts:

### VERIFIED
A claim has public evidence from an accessible source such as a website, official repo, blockchain explorer, marketplace page, archived source, company record, or reputable media source.

### SOURCE NEEDED
A claim is probably true based on project memory or founder statements, but should not be presented as hard public fact until proof is attached.

### INTERNAL CANON
A claim is accepted inside the NO BALL GAMES / Charlie Buster creative universe, but it is lore, brand history, or project positioning rather than independently verified public history.

### DO NOT CLAIM YET
A claim may be commercially sensitive, legally sensitive, or unsupported. It should not be published until verified.

## 5. NO BALL GAMES meaning

### 5.1 Core definition

**Status:** VERIFIED as current website positioning / INTERNAL CANON as deeper ideology

NO BALL GAMES is a phrase from British public signage. It usually appears in spaces where authorities want to stop play: estates, car parks, fenced courts, school walls, parks, and shared urban spaces.

The brand flips that restriction.

The sign says:

> Do not play here.

The brand answers:

> We played anyway.

The sign says:

> You do not belong in this space.

The brand answers:

> We built a culture from this space.

The sign says:

> This area is controlled.

The brand answers:

> This wall is now part of the story.

### 5.2 Brand pillars

NO BALL GAMES can be defined through these pillars:

1. **Restriction** — the original sign, public rules, blocked spaces.
2. **Rebellion** — refusing to let the ban define the person.
3. **Graffiti** — turning surfaces into identity.
4. **Grime energy** — raw UK sound, attitude, pace, and estate-born creativity.
5. **Collector culture** — physical and digital objects with story and scarcity.
6. **Community** — fans, artists, players, collectors, collaborators.
7. **Lore** — characters, restricted zones, secret games, coded signs.
8. **Utility** — future rewards, access, quests, drops, physical/digital links.

### 5.3 Strong homepage phrase

Use this as the master one-liner:

> **NO BALL GAMES turns the sign that told us where not to play into a street-culture brand built on art, rebellion, community, collectibles, and the right to create your own space.**
