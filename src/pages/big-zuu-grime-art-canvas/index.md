In January 2022, Charlie Buster painted a custom Big Zuu canvas — a piece built around music, personality, colour, pressure and the raw energy that sits at the heart of Grime Art.

The canvas took roughly three weeks to complete in Charlie’s study before being delivered in person to Big Zuu’s manager, Matt, at the Ditto office in central London. It was not just another artist portrait. It was another moment where Charlie Buster’s work crossed directly into the world of UK music culture.

Big Zuu is one of the clearest examples of a modern British creative who cannot be boxed into one lane. He is a grime artist, rapper, DJ, TV personality, cook, presenter and cultural figure from West London. He built his name through music, then reached a wider audience through television with *Big Zuu’s Big Eats*, the BAFTA-winning food and entertainment series that brought his personality into homes across the country.

That range makes him a strong subject for Grime Art.

Because Grime Art is not about painting someone flat. It is about painting the energy around them.

## **The Difference Between a Portrait and a Grime Art Portrait**

A traditional portrait usually focuses on likeness, proportion, expression and realism.

A Grime Art portrait goes further.

It still needs the likeness. Without that, the piece fails. But likeness is only the foundation. The real work is in the atmosphere.

Charlie Buster’s Grime Art portraits use colour, movement, layering, symbols, graffiti marks and visual rhythm to build the subject’s identity. The result should feel like more than an image. It should feel like the person has been translated into a street-level visual language.

That matters with Big Zuu.

His public image is not cold or distant. It is expressive, loud, warm, funny and energetic. A plain portrait could easily miss that. Grime Art gives the painting room to carry personality.

The artwork can be bold because Zuu is bold.

It can be colourful because his world is colourful.

It can be loose in places because his energy is not stiff.

That is the point.
