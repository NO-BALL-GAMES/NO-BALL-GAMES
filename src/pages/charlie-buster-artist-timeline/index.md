A chronological record of Charlie Buster’s artistic development, commissions, exhibitions, brand projects and wider creative practice, based on the artist’s supplied timeline.

## 1998 — Born in Kent, England.

From an early age Charlie demonstrated a natural passion for drawing and visual creativity, with photographic evidence documenting artistic activity from infancy.

## 2002–2005 — Early legal graffiti and street-inspired artwork

Began creating legal graffiti and street-inspired artwork. Developed an early fascination with typography, graffiti culture, murals, public art and urban environments.

## 2006–2007 — Independent artwork and early sales

Began producing and selling artwork independently. Started developing a recognisable artistic identity influenced by graffiti, street culture, music and contemporary popular culture.

## 2008 — Graffiti Kings London

Joined Graffiti Kings London at its inception. Worked across public art, commercial murals, workshops, events and creative projects while developing technical painting skills and gaining experience within the professional art industry. First major commercial work undertaken through Graffiti Kings, including projects connected to BBC productions.

## 2008–2019 — Graffiti Kings apprenticeship and professional development

Progressed from in-house artist to senior creative roles involving:

- Commercial mural production
- Public art projects
- Client management
- Workshop delivery
- Brand activations
- Creative consultancy
- Sales and business development
- Marketing and partnerships
- Head of Marketing & Collaborations

Worked on projects for global brands, public institutions, schools, councils, festivals and private clients throughout the UK.

## 2011 — V Festival VIP Area

Created mural artwork for the V Festival VIP Area on behalf of Graffiti Kings. Produced large-scale tropical-themed artwork and immersive event decoration. Later recognised this period as the conceptual birth of what would eventually become the Grime Art movement.

## 2012–2017 — Adidas collaborations

Contributed to multiple campaigns, activations, events and creative projects involving:

- Product launches
- Mural marketing
- Live art
- Street art content creation
- Private events

## 2014–2016 — Nike collaborations

Worked on a variety of campaigns and creative projects including:

- Product launches
- Mural marketing
- Live painting
- Brand activations
- Private events

## June 2016 — DJ Bailey

Created a commissioned portrait artwork celebrating the influential Drum & Bass DJ and broadcaster. First major celebrity artwork commission.

## September 2016 — Suzuki & Ant & Dec

Created a custom portrait artwork commissioned in association with Suzuki featuring television presenters Ant & Dec.

## March 2017 — Section Boys (Smoke Boys)

Created a six-piece portrait series featuring all members of the influential UK rap collective. Also designed and hand-painted six custom Charlie Buster T-shirts worn during promotional photography.

## July 2017 — Supercar Blondie

Produced commissioned portrait artwork celebrating the internationally recognised automotive content creator.

## August 2017 — Furla

Created a collection of hand-painted luxury Furla handbags featuring iconic cultural figures including Bob Marley, Amy Winehouse and other music legends. Commissioned as part of the launch of Furla’s Bicester Village boutique.

## 2017–2019 — HMP Swaleside & HMP Lewes

Produced numerous large-scale murals throughout both correctional facilities. Projects focused on environmental improvement and community engagement through art.

## April 2018 — FLUX Exhibition — Chelsea College of Arts, London

Participated in a major contemporary art group exhibition presented by FLUX Exhibition. First significant gallery exhibition.

## 2018–Present — M20 Graffiti Bridge

Produced numerous large-scale murals and graffiti productions at one of Kent’s best-known legal painting locations.

## February 2019 — Miles Nazaire

Created commissioned portrait artwork featuring the television personality and entrepreneur.

## June 2019 — Greenwich Peninsula Golf Range

Produced three large-scale mural installations for the venue’s restaurant space as part of the FLUX Exhibition programme.

## July 2019 — FLUX Exhibition — Turning Tides Festival

O2 Arena, Greenwich Peninsula, London. Participated in a contemporary group exhibition alongside selected artists.

## Summer 2019 — Gordon Ramsay — Lucky Cat

Hand-painted approximately 300 individual Lucky Cat sculptures for Gordon Ramsay’s Lucky Cat restaurant launch. The project later appeared in the Netflix documentary *Being Gordon Ramsay*. One of the most significant commercial art commissions of Charlie’s career.

## October 2019 — Deno Driz

Produced artwork associated with the launch of the artist’s “Eye 2 Eye” project and accompanying promotional events.

## January 2020 — Leake Street Roof Project

Completed a large-scale ten-metre rooftop artwork at London’s internationally recognised Leake Street graffiti tunnel.

## March 2020 — FLUX Art Placement — Candriam Headquarters

Candriam Headquarters, Barbican, London. Selected for a corporate art placement programme organised by FLUX Exhibition.

## March 2020 — Killa Kela & Lisa Marie

Produced portrait artwork featuring the renowned beatbox artist and presenter alongside Lisa Marie.

## April 2020 — Fancy Alexandersson

Created a four-piece contemporary series reinterpreting photographic imagery through Grime Art techniques and aesthetics.

## March 2020–Present — Aston Merrygold & Family

Produced multiple commissioned artworks and bespoke hand-painted jackets for Aston Merrygold and his family. Ongoing collaboration.

## December 2020 — Lottie Tomlinson & Sue Ryder Charity

Commissioned artwork created to celebrate and announce a partnership involving Lottie Tomlinson and Sue Ryder.

## December 2020 — Knucks

Produced a major Grime Art portrait commission celebrating the acclaimed UK rapper.

## January–February 2021 — Black the Ripper, Screech Tree & Dank of England

Created multiple commemorative artworks honouring Black the Ripper and associated figures from UK cannabis and music culture.

## February 2021–Present — Central Cee

Ongoing collaboration involving multiple original artworks including:

- Portrait paintings
- Wild West inspired artwork
- SYNA related artwork
- Featured artwork appearing in the music video “Ungrateful”

The video has accumulated tens of millions of views globally.

## February 2021 — Grime Gran & Risky Roads

Produced commissioned portrait artwork celebrating the popular online personalities.

## August 2021 — Not3s

## July 2023 — Sevaqk

## October 2023 — Claudia Rose

## July 2024 — FLUX Exhibition — 206 Marylebone Road, London

Participated in a contemporary group exhibition showcasing current works.

## 2020–Present — Web3 & Digital Art

Co-created and helped launch multiple NFT and blockchain projects including:

- Graffiti Kings
- [Crypto Moonboys & Girls](/crypto-moonboys/) — the Graffiti Kings-connected WAX creator umbrella, character identities, wiki and game world
- NoBallGames

Contributed to launches generating approximately $3,000,000 USD in cumulative sales and helped achieve a world-record NFT sales milestone exceeding one million NFTs sold within a 24-hour period.

## Current Practice

Charlie Buster currently works as:

- Contemporary Artist
- Founder of Grime Art
- Mural Artist
- Creative Consultant
- Live Artist
- Brand Activation Specialist
- Public Artist
- Educator & Workshop Leader

Operating across original artworks, murals, sculptures, custom apparel, brand collaborations, public art, private commissions and cultural projects throughout the UK and internationally.

For commissions, available artwork and private enquiries, visit [grimeartist.com](https://grimeartist.com/) or [contact NO BALL GAMES](/contact/).
