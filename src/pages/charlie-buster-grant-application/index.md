## Funding Statement

As the founder of the Grime Art movement, my aim is to preserve, document, and reimagine the visual language of contemporary British street culture through accessible public art, community engagement, and contemporary fine art practice.

Over the past two decades I have worked across public murals, workshops, educational programmes, youth engagement projects, charity initiatives, exhibitions, and commercial commissions throughout the United Kingdom.

My work is rooted in the belief that creativity has the power to transform environments, strengthen communities, improve wellbeing, and create opportunities for people who may not otherwise engage with the arts.

Funding would support the development of new Grime Art projects that combine public engagement, artistic excellence, education, and cultural storytelling.

These projects may include:

- Community murals
- Youth engagement programmes
- Public art installations
- Educational workshops
- Artist talks
- Cultural heritage projects
- Regeneration initiatives
- Exhibition development

The objective is not only to create artwork, but to create meaningful opportunities for participation, learning, community pride, and cultural expression.

Having delivered workshops and creative projects for thousands of young people and adults across schools, youth clubs, community centres, charities, and rehabilitation programmes, I understand the value of art as both a creative and social tool.

This funding would allow Grime Art to reach wider audiences, support emerging creative talent, and continue building a distinctly British contemporary art movement that is accessible, relevant, and culturally significant.
