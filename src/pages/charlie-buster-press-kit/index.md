## Press Introduction

Charlie Buster is a British contemporary artist, muralist, and founder of the Grime Art movement.

From painting graffiti as a child in Kent to working with global brands, celebrities, musicians, galleries, and luxury clients, Charlie has spent more than two decades developing a distinctive artistic style that bridges the worlds of street culture and contemporary fine art.

His work combines graffiti, portraiture, music culture, fashion, typography, luxury aesthetics, and urban storytelling to create bold, collectible artworks that reflect modern British culture.

Today, Charlie’s work can be found on gallery walls, private collections, luxury products, public murals, custom apparel, and commercial commissions throughout the United Kingdom and beyond.

## The Story

Kid from Kent.

Graffiti Kings apprentice.

Artist.

Muralist.

Creative consultant.

Founder of Grime Art.

Charlie’s journey began long before galleries and celebrity commissions.

Growing up surrounded by graffiti, skate culture, music, council estates, train lines, and the visual language of urban Britain, he developed a fascination with the way art could transform ordinary environments into something unforgettable.

In 2008 he joined Graffiti Kings London, one of the world’s leading mural and street art companies.

Over the following decade he progressed from in-house artist to Head of Marketing & Collaborations, helping deliver projects for major brands, public institutions, festivals, schools, councils, and private clients across the UK.

Alongside his commercial career, Charlie continued developing his own artistic voice.

That voice eventually became Grime Art.

A distinctly British contemporary art movement inspired by graffiti, street culture, music, fashion, urban decay, aspiration, and rebellion.

Today, Grime Art represents Charlie’s mission to bring the raw visual language of the streets into the world of contemporary collectible art.

## What Is Grime Art?

Grime Art is the visual language of modern British street culture.

Created by Charlie Buster, Grime Art combines graffiti, street art, music, fashion, urban textures, popular culture, and contemporary fine art into a single artistic movement.

Inspired by the layers of posters, stickers, paint, grime, tags, and stories found throughout Britain’s towns and cities, Grime Art takes the raw energy of the street and reimagines it for galleries, collectors, luxury spaces, and contemporary audiences.

It is unapologetically British.

Raw.

Layered.

Authentic.

Collectible.

## Short Press Bio

Charlie Buster is a British contemporary artist, muralist, and founder of the Grime Art movement. Beginning his creative journey through graffiti culture before joining Graffiti Kings London in 2008, Charlie has spent more than two decades developing a body of work that combines street culture, music, fashion, and contemporary fine art. His clients and collaborators have included Gordon Ramsay, Central Cee, Aston Merrygold, Nike, Adidas, Furla, Giggs, Kidd Keo, and numerous private collectors. Today, Charlie continues to create original artworks, murals, public art projects, and cultural collaborations while building Grime Art into a recognised contemporary art movement.
